About me

The nickname Michtah is a shorthand for my unofficial nickname. GitHub chose it for me when I was creating this account, and I think it kind of looks cool, so I decided to keep it for good. That's a little fact about my name.

I am currently a student at the University of York, studying Computer Science as an International Year One student. I would describe myself as a programmer, artist, and music producer. I also do writing for fun but I wouldn't consider myself to be an actual writer as I just write short stories as ideas for my drawings.

My favourite colour is gold. Could you tell?

Interests

I have quite a lot of interests. I keep on collecting them and I really cannot stop. While with some of these I am quite proficient, with others I'd consider myself to only be a novice. Here they are:

  • Programming: Probably the most obvious of these interests. I love programming. I started learning it all the way back in middle school when I was just ten years old. I started out with Python, which I used all the way until my sophomore year of high school, where I joined the Turkish Computer Science Olympiad and so I started learning C instead. I learned C for two years. Now in York, I am currently learning Java and some NASM. In my free time, I've also learned how to program in APL.
  • Art: My largest interest besides Programming, I'd have to say, but I love all my interests all the same, like a loving mother would. Anyways, I quite like art. I plan on hosting a portfolio of art maybe later, but I am quite concerned about web scrapers having a looksy without my permission. Maybe when I implement some more security features and start self-hosting this website, I'll actually get around to that. For now, just know I like drawing, I have an art tablet. I use Krita and I am sometimes out there in Magma under various unrelated nicknames.
  • Mathematics: I love mathematics, and to be honest I think I like it more than programming even (not to say I don't like programming), but this field feels so much more vast and available for exploration than programming and computer science. I am a member of the university's math society, and I like to talk to people on there and exchange ideas. I love all kinds of maths (except statistics). But my favourite fields are set theory, ordinal analysis, logic, number theory, and philosophy of mathematics. I love talking about it with others, even if I really don't measure up when it comes to knowledge.
  • Music: I love music. I am listening to music and I am writing this text. I love all kinds of music, but I especially like electronic music. I used to like very hard dance music, but recently I've been gravitating towards groovy house / french house music. I also like hard techno when the mood fits. I also make music, mainly of the latter kind. I find it quite fun to make. I sometimes share it with my online friends but I haven't really posted anything so far.
So yeah, those are my interests. If you like them, let me know!
