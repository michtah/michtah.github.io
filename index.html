About me

The nickname Michtah is a shorthand for my unofficial nickname. GitHub chose it for me when I was creating this account, and I think it kind of looks cool, so I decided to keep it for good. That's a little fact about my name.

I am currently a student at the University of York, studying Computer Science as an International Year One student. I would describe myself as a programmer, artist, and music producer. I also do writing for fun but I wouldn't consider myself to be an actual writer as I just write short stories as ideas for my drawings.

My favourite colour is gold. Could you tell?

Interests

I have quite a lot of interests. I keep on collecting them and I really cannot stop. While with some of these I am quite proficient, with others I'd consider myself to only be a novice. Here they are:

  • Programming: I like programming because you can do things with it that you never could otherwise. I started learning how to code in middle school with Python but later learned languages like C as part of my training for the computer science olympiad. I am currently learning Java and NASM.
  • Art: My largest interest besides programming. I love making art of all kinds from digital paintings to technical diagrams to fantasy mapmaking. I will host a portfolio specifically for my art projects down the line, but first I want to make sure nothing can scrape it. I mainly use Krita and Paint.NET if I'm on Windows.
  • Mathematics: I have been interested in maths from a very young age. I entered various maths competitions especially in middle school. It's what evolved into my current interests in programming. I'd consider myself a decent set theorist.
  • Music: I love listening to music and thinking about it. I listen to music probably several hours a day. I love all kinds of music, but I mostly listen to electronic music like techno. I make music, mostly experimental electronic stuff related to programming.
So yeah, those are my interests. If you like them, let me know!
