March 4th: Remaking Michtah

Hello. You are here. In my production diary. I'm going to be talking about how I improved my website to fit the ten usability heuristics. It was an interesting challenged and I did a lot of changes, which is what I'll be talking about.

The state of Michtah before

The website before was quite messy because it was hastily created for the sake of just having it done for class. The styling was inconsistent and I feel like the portfolio page was too flashy with a lot of animations that just made it tiring to look at. In particular, the most violated heuristics were probably:

  • User control and freedom: because the user had no way of navigating the pages previously.
  • Consistency and standards: because the website felt like a mish-mash of various quick hacks.
  • Aesthetic and minimalist design: the styling was either non-existent or garish and over the top.
  • Along with some more minor infractions of other heuristics, but these three were the main ones.

The website really was not nice to look at or use. So I did something about it. I rewrote it scratch. This was a big endeavour and it took me a few days, but I managed to get it to look like this in the end. I really had a good time rewriting it, seeing it work well like this. I am quite happy with the appearance of it. The style is easy to look at, but I also decided to avoid the flat and boring designs that people have been making recently (see the headers and their shiny appearance).

Another thing was the navigation menu. It was quite an effort to get them to look that good and also to stay on top. I learned about z-depth, as without it the navigation bar would collide with the text below it. I added various navigational tools into it.

I would say all of these have culminated in a much better user experience where the users can browse my site freely and look around without getting stuck on dead ends or broken links. It was a good learning experience in how to style a page, how to design a layout, and how to make a navigation bar. I had a good time.

That's all for now.
