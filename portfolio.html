About Me

I am Michael Tahiroğlu. I am studying Computer Science in the University of York as an International Year One student.

I'm mainly interested in computation for simulations and mathematics, as well as graphic design and programming. I want to write code that helps us understand our world.

Skills

Programming: I have intermediate knowledge in Python, Java, and C. I also know some GLSL, APL, and NASM. Furthermore I can also use HTML/CSS/JS. I was part of the Computer Science Olympiad of Turkey for two years.

Spoken Languages: I am a native in Turkish, but better in spoken Turkish with some good technical vocabulary in Mathematics. I also speak English as a second language. I have an IELTS score of 8.5 and a C1 CEFR band. I have a wider vocabulary in English, especially for technical terms. Beyond those two I know a bit of German and Finnish.

Personal Interests: I have been drawing for six years now. Digitally I use Krita to draw. I also like mathematics, especially logic and set theory. I also make some experimental electronic music from time to time.
