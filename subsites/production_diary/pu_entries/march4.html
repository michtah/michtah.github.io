March 4th: Remaking Michtah

Hello. This diary entry is going to talk about the redesign of the website into the design that you're seeing right now. I did this as part of my assignment to fit the website better into the 10 heuristics of usability. The website was very inconsistent and half-finished initially, and each page had it's own styling with a completely different (and other times non-existent) look. I feel as though I did a good job.

One part I am particularly proud of is the navigation bar. For now, I am adding the whole navigation system manually, but later on I'd like to have a more automatic way of doing this. Regardless, I think it is pretty cool and useful for navigating from and into pages. One thing I'd like to add is the ability to see the link someone usually might want to access from the current page. For example, the production diary wouldn't have every single diary entry in it's links area, but the main page might have some quick navigation buttons at the top.

Another aspect is making the navigation stay at the top. I learned what z-values are, what a fixed position is, and some other things to make it work. It was an interesting challenge.

So, what's left to do? I think the main thing I have to do is add more pages. I need to re-introduce the large numbers section. However, I did make this page in its place in a way. I'm also planning on styling that page differently.
